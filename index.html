About

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), India.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Dinabandhu Pradhan.

My research lies in graph theory, which is a part of combinatorics and theoretical computer science. I am particularly interested in structural graph theory, domination problems, graph coloring, and pursuit-evasion games on graphs.

Research

My research focuses on structural properties of graphs and their applications to problems involving graph parameters. In particular, I am interested in the following topics:

  • Domination in Graphs: Domination and its variants, including secure domination, paired domination, double domination, disjunctive domination, and locating-dominating problems.
  • Structural Graph Theory: Structural properties and parameter bounds in graph classes defined by forbidden induced subgraphs.
  • Graph Coloring: Coloring problems and structural aspects of restricted graph classes.
  • Pursuit-Evasion Games: Cops and Robbers games and related pursuit-evasion problems on graphs.
