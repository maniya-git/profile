Welcome

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi).

My research is in graph theory, a branch of combinatorics and theoretical computer science. I am particularly interested in structural graph theory and domination problems, including relationships between graph parameters under structural restrictions on graphs.

Research Interests

  • Domination and related parameters in graphs.
  • Structural graph theory and forbidden induced subgraphs.
  • Graph coloring and related problems.
  • Pursuit-evasion games on graphs, including Cops and Robbers.
