Welcome to my personal webpage. I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Mathematics, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, working with Prof. Dalu Jacob.

My research is in structural graph theory, particularly domination problems. I am also interested in pursuit-evasion games on graphs and graph coloring.

About

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad in 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Dinabandhu Pradhan.

Before joining IIT Delhi, I visited the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati for three months, working with Prof. Sriram Bhyravarapu.

Research Interests

My work focuses on structural properties of graphs and the relationships between graph parameters, especially under local structural restrictions such as forbidden induced subgraphs.
