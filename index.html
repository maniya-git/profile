About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. I work in graph theory, a part of combinatorics and theoretical computer science.

My research focuses on structural and algorithmic aspects of graphs, particularly domination parameters, forbidden induced-subgraph classes, graph colouring, and pursuit-evasion games.

Research

My interests lie in structural graph theory, with an emphasis on how local restrictions on a graph influence global parameters and algorithmic properties.
